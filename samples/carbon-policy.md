# Carbon Policy 2026

## Section 1: Goals

The corporation commits to achieving net-zero greenhouse-gas emissions across
all operations by 2050. Interim milestones target a 50% reduction by 2035 and
a 75% reduction by 2042 relative to the 2020 baseline.

## Section 2: Implementation Timeline

Phase one launches in Q1 2026 and covers facility-level energy retrofits and
the transition of company vehicles to electric drivetrains. Phase two runs
from Q3 2027 through Q4 2029 and tackles supply-chain decarbonization,
including supplier reporting requirements and a carbon-budget allocation
program for purchasing teams. Phase three, covering 2030 to 2042, executes
the scope-3 strategy and the renewable-PPA portfolio buildout.

### Subsection 2.1: Reporting

All scopes are reported quarterly to the board and annually to shareholders.
The Chief Sustainability Officer is responsible for assurance and external
audit.

## Section 3: Budget

Capital allocation for 2026 is fifty million dollars, increasing on a
declining schedule through 2030 as efficiency gains compound.

## Section 4: Governance

The Sustainability Committee meets monthly. Material exceptions require board
approval. Compliance is mandatory and is reflected in executive compensation.
